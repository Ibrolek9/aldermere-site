The Cormorant House

Sag Harbor, New York

Set on a quiet finger of Sag Harbor's shoreline, the Cormorant House pairs its 1932 cedar-shingled bones with a considered 2021 renovation — wide-board oak floors, a kitchen built around a single 14-foot island, and a wall of glass facing the water. The dock accommodates a boat up to 32 feet; the lawn runs flat to the water's edge.
